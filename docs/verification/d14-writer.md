- Loose teacher sentences (e.g. "wann darfst du von Berlin abfahren") not reproduced; own correct examples used.
- "Überraschung" vs "Überraschungsparty": only Überraschung / Überraschungstag are used.
- Lowercase after "Lieber Max," is the traditional rule; stated with its exception (nouns, Sie).
