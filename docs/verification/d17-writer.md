## Differences between the teacher and standard German
- Teacher: "ihrer Kind" (her child) -> standard "ihrem Kind" (neuter dative); corrected in a compare row.
- Teacher: "Auto is feminine, so dem" — das Auto is neuter (dem is right for neuter too). The Day uses das Auto.
- Teacher: Bücherei = bookshop. Standard: Bücherei = public library; Buchhandlung = bookshop. Corrected.
- Teacher: "zu is for places within a city, nach for cities". Written as the standard rule: zu = place or person, nach = city/country; plus zu Hause / nach Hause.
- Teacher: "von is used when you cannot see the person coming out". Replaced by the standard pairs zu -> von, in -> aus.
- Teacher: "with these verbs the object is always dative; all other verbs accusative". Softened: a small group takes the dative, more follow later, geben takes both.
- "reden vs sprechen" explanation was loose and is not reproduced.
